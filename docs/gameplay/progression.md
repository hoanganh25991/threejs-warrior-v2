# Character Progression

## Attribute System

### Primary Attributes
- **Strength**: Increases physical damage, health, and carrying capacity
- **Agility**: Increases attack speed, dodge chance, and movement speed
- **Intelligence**: Increases magical damage, mana pool, and spell effectiveness
- **Vitality**: Increases health regeneration, status resistance, and maximum health
- **Spirit**: Increases resource regeneration, cooldown reduction, and critical chance

### Derived Statistics
- **Health**: Base value + (Strength × 10) + (Vitality × 15)
- **Mana/Energy**: Base value + (Intelligence × 8) + (Spirit × 5)
- **Physical Damage**: Base value + (Primary attribute × 2)
- **Magical Damage**: Base value + (Intelligence × 1.5)
- **Attack Speed**: Base value + (Agility × 0.5%)
- **Movement Speed**: Base value + (Agility × 0.3%)
- **Dodge Chance**: Base value + (Agility × 0.2%)
- **Critical Chance**: Base value + (Spirit × 0.3%)
- **Cooldown Reduction**: Base value + (Spirit × 0.5%)

### Attribute Growth
- Each hero has unique base attributes and growth rates
- Attributes increase automatically with level
- Equipment provides additional attribute bonuses
- Temporary buffs can enhance attributes for limited periods
- Certain talents can permanently increase attribute growth rates

## Level Progression

### Experience Requirements
- Level 1-10: 1,000 XP per level
- Level 11-20: 2,000 XP per level
- Level 21-30: 3,000 XP per level
- Total XP required for maximum level: 60,000

### Level-up Rewards
- Level 2-4: 1 Ability Point
- Level 5: 1 Ability Point, 1 Talent Point
- Level 6-9: 1 Ability Point
- Level 10: 1 Ability Point, 1 Talent Point
- Level 11-14: 1 Ability Point
- Level 15: 1 Ability Point, 1 Talent Point
- Level 16-19: 1 Ability Point
- Level 20: 1 Ability Point, 1 Talent Point
- Level 21-24: 1 Ability Point
- Level 25: 1 Ability Point, 1 Talent Point
- Level 26-30: 1 Ability Point

### Experience Sources
- **Enemy Defeat**: 10-500 XP based on enemy level and type
- **Quest Completion**: 100-2,000 XP based on difficulty
- **Area Discovery**: 50-200 XP for finding new locations
- **Lore Collection**: 25-100 XP for finding lore items
- **Boss Defeat**: 1,000-5,000 XP for major encounters
- **Crafting**: Small XP rewards for creating new items

## Ability Advancement

### Ability Points
- Earned primarily through level-up (1 per level)
- Additional points from special quests and rare items
- Maximum of 40 ability points available per hero
- Points can be redistributed at special locations for a cost

### Ability Tiers
Each ability has three tiers of advancement:

#### Tier 1 (1 Point)
- Base functionality of the ability
- Moderate damage/effect
- Standard cooldown and resource cost

#### Tier 2 (2 Additional Points)
- Enhanced damage/effect (approximately +50%)
- Reduced cooldown (approximately -20%)
- Additional minor effect (e.g., slow, brief stun)
- Improved area of effect or range

#### Tier 3 (3 Additional Points)
- Significantly enhanced damage/effect (approximately +100% from base)
- Further reduced cooldown (approximately -40% from base)
- Additional major effect (e.g., piercing damage, longer stun)
- Fundamental improvement to ability functionality

### Ability Modifications
- Special items can permanently modify abilities
- Certain talents can alter how abilities function
- Environmental factors can temporarily enhance abilities
- Hero-specific quests can unlock hidden potential in abilities

## Talent System

### Talent Structure
- Three specialization paths per hero
- Five talent tiers unlocked at levels 5, 10, 15, 20, and 25
- One point to spend at each tier
- Points can be redistributed at special locations for a cost

### Talent Paths
Each hero has three distinct talent paths representing different playstyles:

#### Path 1: Offensive Focus
- Emphasizes damage output and aggressive playstyle
- Examples: increased critical damage, penetrating attacks, reduced offensive cooldowns

#### Path 2: Defensive Focus
- Emphasizes survivability and control
- Examples: damage reduction, crowd control duration, shield generation

#### Path 3: Utility Focus
- Emphasizes versatility and resource management
- Examples: cooldown reduction, resource regeneration, movement abilities

### Talent Types
- **Passive Enhancements**: Permanent stat increases or new passive effects
- **Ability Modifications**: Changes to how specific abilities function
- **New Abilities**: Entirely new active or passive abilities
- **Playstyle Modifiers**: Fundamental changes to hero mechanics
- **Resource Alterations**: Changes to resource generation or consumption

## Reputation System

### Faction Reputation
- Reputation tracks with major factions (Sentinel, Scourge, Neutrals)
- Five reputation tiers: Hostile, Unfriendly, Neutral, Friendly, Exalted
- Higher reputation unlocks special vendors, quests, and areas
- Actions in the world affect reputation dynamically
- Reputation with one faction may affect others (positively or negatively)

### NPC Relationships
- Individual relationship tracking with key NPCs
- Relationship affects dialogue options and quest availability
- Gifts can improve relationships
- Certain choices can permanently damage relationships
- High relationship levels unlock personal quests and unique rewards

## Achievement System

### Achievement Categories
- **Combat Mastery**: Achievements for combat prowess
- **Explorer**: Achievements for discovering locations and secrets
- **Collector**: Achievements for gathering items and lore
- **Hero Journey**: Character-specific achievements
- **Faction Champion**: Achievements related to faction reputation
- **Master Craftsman**: Achievements for crafting and upgrading items

### Achievement Rewards
- Cosmetic rewards (character appearances, effects)
- Titles displayed in-game
- Minor gameplay bonuses
- Unique equipment
- Ability modifications
- Access to secret areas
