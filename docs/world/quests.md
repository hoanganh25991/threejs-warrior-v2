# Quests & Mission Structure

## Quest Types

### Main Quests

#### Campaign Structure
- Linear main storyline divided into four acts
- Each act contains 5-7 main quests
- Main quests advance the central narrative and unlock new areas
- Critical choices in main quests can affect later story developments
- Main quests often feature unique gameplay mechanics and set pieces

#### Act Progression
- **Act 1: Fractured Realms**
  - Introduces the world, factions, and initial conflict
  - Culminates in the discovery of the greater threat
  - Unlocks hero switching mechanic
- **Act 2: Ancient Awakening**
  - Expands the world map significantly
  - Focuses on gathering information about the ancient threat
  - Introduces faction reputation system
- **Act 3: The Gathering Storm**
  - Focuses on collecting the Fragments of Creation
  - Highest concentration of faction conflict and choices
  - Unlocks advanced abilities and talents
- **Act 4: The Final Seal**
  - Brings all story threads together
  - Features the most challenging combat encounters
  - Leads to multiple possible endings based on previous choices

#### Key Decision Points
- Whether to prioritize faction interests or the greater good
- Which allies to recruit and support
- How to use limited resources (save one area at the expense of another)
- Moral choices about using forbidden knowledge or power

### Side Quests

#### Regional Side Quests
- 10-15 side quests per major region
- Self-contained stories that expand world lore
- Often feature unique characters not seen in the main campaign
- Rewards include unique equipment, ability modifications, and reputation

#### Chains and Series
- Multi-part quest chains that tell extended stories
- Quests that change over time as the world state changes
- Interconnected quests where choices in one affect others
- Quest series that can only be advanced at certain times or under certain conditions

#### Discovery Quests
- Initiated by finding items or locations rather than NPCs
- Environmental puzzles that lead to hidden rewards
- Treasure hunts following clues scattered throughout the world
- Archaeological expeditions to piece together ancient history

### Hero Quests

#### Personal Storylines
- Each hero has a 5-part personal quest chain
- Explores the hero's backstory and motivations
- Presents moral choices that reflect the hero's character development
- Rewards include hero-specific equipment and ability modifications

#### Hero-Specific Challenges
- Combat trials tailored to each hero's abilities
- Puzzles that require creative use of hero mechanics
- Timed challenges that test mastery of the hero's kit
- Special encounters with NPCs who have history with the hero

#### Rivalry and Relationship Quests
- Quests involving multiple heroes that develop their relationships
- Rivalry quests where heroes compete for resources or recognition
- Cooperation quests that require switching between heroes
- Romance options for certain hero pairings with compatible stories

### Repeatable Quests

#### Daily Challenges
- Rotating set of 3-5 daily quests
- Focus on core gameplay loops (combat, exploration, collection)
- Rewards include crafting materials and reputation
- Different challenges for different hero types

#### Weekly Expeditions
- More substantial repeatable content refreshed weekly
- Mini-dungeons with randomized elements
- Special boss encounters with varying mechanics
- Rewards include rare equipment and ability modifications

#### Bounty System
- Procedurally generated hunting quests
- Target creatures or NPCs appear in random locations
- Difficulty tiers with escalating rewards
- Special "notorious" targets with unique abilities and drops

## Objective Types

### Combat Objectives

#### Defeat Enemies
- Standard combat encounters with specific enemy types
- Kill counts (defeat X number of enemies)
- Elite targets with special abilities
- Boss encounters with unique mechanics

#### Defend Locations
- Hold position against waves of enemies
- Protect NPCs during attacks
- Maintain control of strategic points
- Escort vulnerable characters through hostile territory

#### Arena Challenges
- Survival against increasingly difficult waves
- Time trials to defeat enemies quickly
- Limited resource challenges (no healing, etc.)
- Special rule sets that modify combat mechanics

### Exploration Objectives

#### Discover Locations
- Find landmarks in the open world
- Uncover hidden areas through environmental puzzles
- Map unexplored territories
- Reach difficult-to-access viewpoints

#### Navigate Hazards
- Traverse dangerous terrain
- Overcome environmental obstacles
- Survive extreme conditions (blizzards, etc.)
- Find safe paths through corrupted areas

#### Investigate Phenomena
- Study unusual magical occurrences
- Track strange creatures
- Document environmental changes
- Research ancient technology

### Collection Objectives

#### Gather Resources
- Harvest natural materials
- Mine rare minerals
- Collect magical essences
- Salvage technology from ruins

#### Recover Artifacts
- Find ancient relics
- Retrieve stolen items
- Collect fragments of powerful objects
- Secure dangerous artifacts before others do

#### Document Knowledge
- Collect pages of scattered texts
- Record oral histories from NPCs
- Transcribe ancient inscriptions
- Gather research data

### Social Objectives

#### Diplomatic Missions
- Negotiate between conflicting groups
- Represent faction interests
- Mediate disputes
- Establish alliances

#### Information Gathering
- Eavesdrop on conversations
- Interrogate captured enemies
- Extract information from reluctant sources
- Verify conflicting reports

#### Reputation Building
- Perform tasks to improve standing with factions
- Participate in cultural ceremonies
- Prove worthiness through trials
- Make public choices that align with faction values

## Quest Design

### Quest Structure

#### Introduction
- Quest giver introduces the situation
- Clear establishment of motivation and stakes
- Initial objective is provided
- Relevant background information is available through dialogue options

#### Progression
- Multiple steps with clear guidance
- Unexpected developments that add complexity
- Branching paths based on player choices
- Opportunities for different approaches (combat, stealth, diplomacy)

#### Resolution
- Meaningful conclusion that acknowledges player choices
- Rewards that feel appropriate to the effort required
- Impact on the world or characters is shown
- Setup for potential follow-up quests

### Narrative Integration

#### World Building
- Quests reveal aspects of the world's history and culture
- Environmental storytelling complements quest narratives
- Faction perspectives provide different views on the same events
- Connections between seemingly unrelated quests reveal larger patterns

#### Character Development
- NPCs grow and change based on quest outcomes
- Heroes respond differently to similar situations
- Relationships evolve through shared experiences
- Personal growth is reflected in dialogue and abilities

#### Thematic Elements
- Quests explore the game's central themes
- Moral choices reflect the complexity of the world
- Cultural differences between factions are highlighted
- Philosophical questions about power, responsibility, and unity

### Player Agency

#### Meaningful Choices
- Decisions with significant consequences
- Multiple valid approaches to objectives
- Moral dilemmas without clear "right" answers
- Opportunities to express character personality

#### Consequence System
- World state changes based on quest outcomes
- NPC reactions differ based on previous choices
- Some areas become accessible or inaccessible
- Future quests are affected by past decisions

#### Failure States
- Quests can be failed but the game continues
- Alternative paths open when traditional approaches fail
- Consequences for failure that impact the world
- Opportunities to address failures in different ways

## Quest Tracking

### Quest Log

#### Organization
- Categorized by main, side, hero, and repeatable quests
- Sortable by region, difficulty, and reward type
- Search functionality for specific quest names or keywords
- Completed quest archive with outcome summaries

#### Detail Levels
- Brief overview of current objective
- Detailed quest description with background
- History of completed steps
- Related quests and connections

#### Tracking Features
- Active quest highlighting
- Multiple quest tracking (up to 3 simultaneously)
- Progress indicators for multi-part objectives
- Time remaining for time-limited quests

### World Interaction

#### Map Markers
- Distinct icons for different quest types
- Objective area indicators rather than exact points for exploration quests
- Toggle options for different quest categories
- Custom markers players can add

#### Environmental Guides
- Subtle visual cues in the environment (e.g., worn paths)
- NPC behaviors that guide players (looking or pointing at points of interest)
- Natural landmarks that serve as navigation aids
- Ambient sounds that indicate nearby objectives

#### Hint System
- Optional hints for stuck players
- Escalating detail levels (vague direction → specific instructions)
- Context-sensitive help based on player actions
- Cooldown timer to prevent over-reliance

### Accessibility Features

#### Difficulty Options
- Adjustable combat challenge in quest encounters
- Extra guidance options for complex navigation
- Extended timers for time-limited objectives
- Simplified puzzle variants

#### Alternative Approaches
- Combat alternatives for most objectives
- Multiple solution paths for puzzles
- Options that accommodate different playstyles
- Bypass options for particularly challenging content

#### Assistance Settings
- Enhanced visual cues for important objects
- Audio cues for nearby objectives
- Auto-navigation option for long travel segments
- Simplified control schemes for complex interactions
