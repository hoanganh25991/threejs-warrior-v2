# NPCs

## Faction Characters

### Sentinel Representatives

#### High Sage Luminara
- **Role**: Leader of the Sentinel Council
- **Appearance**: Elderly elven woman with silver hair and glowing blue eyes, dressed in flowing robes with nature motifs
- **Personality**: Wise, patient, but sometimes rigid in her adherence to tradition
- **Quests**: Provides main quest guidance and faction-specific missions
- **Special Interactions**: Can enhance Sentinel heroes' abilities through blessing rituals

#### Commander Thorian
- **Role**: Military leader of Sentinel forces
- **Appearance**: Muscular human with battle scars and ornate armor featuring the Sentinel emblem
- **Personality**: Honorable, strategic, suspicious of the Scourge
- **Quests**: Combat-focused missions against Scourge forces and hostile creatures
- **Special Interactions**: Provides combat training that grants temporary buffs

#### Archivist Sylvanas
- **Role**: Keeper of Sentinel knowledge and history
- **Appearance**: Young elven woman with spectacles and practical robes covered in pockets full of scrolls
- **Personality**: Curious, enthusiastic about learning, open-minded about the Scourge
- **Quests**: Research missions to recover lost knowledge
- **Special Interactions**: Translates ancient texts that reveal secret locations

### Scourge Representatives

#### Overlord Malachai
- **Role**: Supreme ruler of the Scourge territories
- **Appearance**: Imposing figure in spiked armor with a skull helmet that conceals his face
- **Personality**: Ruthless, pragmatic, but with a hidden code of honor
- **Quests**: Provides main quest guidance from the Scourge perspective
- **Special Interactions**: Can grant Scourge heroes temporary powers through blood rituals

#### Mistress Vex
- **Role**: Spymaster and assassin trainer
- **Appearance**: Lithe woman with pale skin, dark clothing, and numerous hidden weapons
- **Personality**: Calculating, sarcastic, values results over methods
- **Quests**: Stealth and assassination missions
- **Special Interactions**: Teaches special stealth techniques

#### Artificer Zul'jin
- **Role**: Master of Scourge technology and dark magic
- **Appearance**: Hunched troll with mechanical augmentations and glowing tattoos
- **Personality**: Brilliant, eccentric, obsessed with innovation
- **Quests**: Gathering rare materials for experiments
- **Special Interactions**: Enhances equipment with unique properties

## Neutral Entities

### Merchants

#### Trader Jax
- **Role**: Traveling merchant who appears in various locations
- **Appearance**: Portly human with a massive backpack and colorful clothing
- **Personality**: Jovial, opportunistic, has connections everywhere
- **Wares**: General supplies, maps, information
- **Special Feature**: Inventory changes based on location and player reputation

#### Artificer Tinka
- **Role**: Master craftswoman who can enhance equipment
- **Appearance**: Gnome with mechanical arm and eye, always covered in soot
- **Personality**: Perfectionist, blunt, charges premium prices
- **Services**: Equipment upgrades, custom crafting
- **Special Feature**: Can add unique enchantments based on rare materials

#### The Collector
- **Role**: Dealer in rare and magical items
- **Appearance**: Mysterious figure in elaborate robes that conceal their identity
- **Personality**: Formal, knowledgeable, slightly unsettling
- **Wares**: Legendary items, ability modifications, rare crafting materials
- **Special Feature**: Accepts unusual forms of payment beyond gold

### Service Providers

#### Innkeeper Mira
- **Role**: Proprietor of inns in major settlements
- **Appearance**: Middle-aged human woman with a warm smile and practical clothing
- **Personality**: Motherly, gossipy, knows everyone's business
- **Services**: Rest (health restoration), food buffs, local information
- **Special Feature**: Provides unique quests based on rumors she's heard

#### Stablemaster Kodo
- **Role**: Manages travel networks between settlements
- **Appearance**: Burly orc with a way with animals
- **Personality**: Gruff but fair, takes pride in his mounts
- **Services**: Fast travel, mount rentals, mount care
- **Special Feature**: Can train special mounts with unique abilities

#### Healer Alaria
- **Role**: Wandering healer who helps both factions
- **Appearance**: Serene woman with white hair despite her youth
- **Personality**: Compassionate, neutral in the conflict, hides a tragic past
- **Services**: Healing, curse removal, status effect cures
- **Special Feature**: Provides unique healing items not available elsewhere

### Quest Givers

#### Professor Whizzle
- **Role**: Eccentric researcher studying ancient civilizations
- **Appearance**: Elderly gnome with wild hair and multiple pairs of spectacles
- **Personality**: Absent-minded, enthusiastic, oblivious to danger
- **Quest Types**: Exploration, artifact recovery, creature study
- **Special Feature**: Rewards include unique knowledge that unlocks secret areas

#### Bounty Hunter Vex
- **Role**: Tracker of dangerous creatures and criminals
- **Appearance**: Scarred human with leather armor and trophy necklaces
- **Personality**: Terse, professional, respects worthy prey
- **Quest Types**: Hunting dangerous creatures, capturing outlaws
- **Special Feature**: Bounties increase in difficulty and reward as reputation grows

#### Elder Moonshadow
- **Role**: Spiritual leader seeking balance between factions
- **Appearance**: Ancient tauren with ceremonial dress and tribal markings
- **Personality**: Wise, speaks in riddles, sees beyond the physical world
- **Quest Types**: Spirit quests, moral choices, faction diplomacy
- **Special Feature**: Choices in his quests affect the world in subtle but important ways

## Hostile Creatures

### Wildlife

#### Dire Wolves
- **Behavior**: Hunt in packs, surround prey, retreat when alpha is killed
- **Abilities**: Howl (summons reinforcements), Pounce (gap closer), Maul (bleeding effect)
- **Weaknesses**: Fire damage, vulnerable when separated from pack
- **Drops**: Wolf pelts, fangs (crafting materials)

#### Giant Spiders
- **Behavior**: Set web traps, ambush from above, protect egg sacs
- **Abilities**: Web Shot (immobilize), Venom Bite (poison DoT), Skitter (quick retreat)
- **Weaknesses**: Ice damage slows them, fire burns webs instantly
- **Drops**: Spider silk, venom sacs (alchemy ingredients)

#### Thunder Lizards
- **Behavior**: Territorial, charge intruders, use terrain for advantage
- **Abilities**: Lightning Breath (chain lightning), Tail Swipe (knockback), Thunderous Stomp (AoE stun)
- **Weaknesses**: Exposed underbelly, vulnerable after charging
- **Drops**: Scales (armor crafting), electric glands (enchanting)

### Humanoid Enemies

#### Bandit Clans
- **Types**: Scouts, Brutes, Archers, Shamans
- **Tactics**: Ambushes, hit-and-run attacks, use of terrain
- **Special Units**: Bandit Chiefs (elite enemies with unique abilities)
- **Drops**: Stolen goods, weapons, maps to hideouts

#### Cult of the Void
- **Types**: Acolytes, Summoners, Void-Touched (corrupted humans)
- **Tactics**: Sacrificial magic, summoning void creatures, mind control
- **Special Units**: Void Prophets (spellcasters with reality-warping abilities)
- **Drops**: Void fragments, forbidden tomes, corrupted artifacts

#### Tribal Gnolls
- **Types**: Hunters, Shamans, Berserkers, Pack Lords
- **Tactics**: Overwhelming numbers, frenzy at low health, totemic buffs
- **Special Units**: Gnoll Matriarchs (command abilities and powerful magic)
- **Drops**: Tribal fetishes, rare furs, ancient tribal artifacts

### Faction-Specific Enemies

#### Sentinel Enemies
- **Radiant Guardians**: Constructs gone rogue after corruption
- **Feral Druids**: Nature-wielders who lost control of their powers
- **Splinter Cell**: Extremist Sentinel faction opposed to peace

#### Scourge Enemies
- **Failed Experiments**: Results of magical research gone wrong
- **Rogue Necromancers**: Death mages pursuing forbidden knowledge
- **Blood Cult**: Extremist Scourge faction that worships ancient entities

### Elite Enemies

#### Ancient Guardians
- **Description**: Stone constructs that protect ancient ruins
- **Special Mechanics**: Invulnerable until their power source is destroyed
- **Unique Abilities**: Seismic Slam (ground-based AoE), Petrifying Gaze (temporary stun)
- **Rewards**: Guardian Cores (rare crafting material), ancient technology

#### Corrupted Champions
- **Description**: Former heroes corrupted by void energy
- **Special Mechanics**: Mimic abilities of playable heroes
- **Unique Abilities**: Void Shift (teleportation), Corruption Aura (DoT to nearby players)
- **Rewards**: Corrupted equipment that can be purified, memory fragments

#### Elemental Lords
- **Description**: Powerful beings composed of pure elemental energy
- **Special Mechanics**: Immune to their own element, vulnerable to opposing elements
- **Unique Abilities**: Element-specific ultimate abilities, environmental manipulation
- **Rewards**: Elemental cores, unique ability modifications

## Bosses

### Dungeon Bosses

#### The Curator
- **Location**: The Forgotten Library
- **Description**: Massive construct made of animated books and artifacts
- **Phases**: 
  1. Knowledge Seeker: Summons book minions and tests players with riddles
  2. Forbidden Archive: Unleashes dangerous knowledge in the form of area attacks
  3. Final Cataloguing: Attempts to "archive" the heroes by trapping them in books
- **Special Mechanics**: Puzzle elements required to make vulnerable
- **Unique Rewards**: Tome of Forgotten Knowledge (ability modification item)

#### Hydromancer Theseus
- **Location**: The Sunken Temple
- **Description**: Former Sentinel mage corrupted by ancient water magic
- **Phases**:
  1. Tidal Master: Controls water to create waves and currents
  2. Ice Form: Freezes the arena and attacks with ice spikes
  3. Steam Wraith: Transforms into mist and creates scalding steam jets
- **Special Mechanics**: Environment changes with each phase
- **Unique Rewards**: Trident of the Tides (legendary weapon)

### Regional Bosses

#### Cindermaw the Untamed
- **Location**: Blightwood (Scourge Territory)
- **Description**: Massive corrupted wolf with flaming fur and multiple heads
- **Phases**:
  1. The Hunt: Stalks players through a burning forest
  2. Frenzy: Direct assault with fire breath and claw attacks
  3. Pack Alpha: Summons corrupted wolf minions
- **Special Mechanics**: Fire trails that persist in the environment
- **Unique Rewards**: Cindermaw's Pelt (legendary armor material)

#### The Ancient Treant
- **Location**: Whispering Glades (Sentinel Territory)
- **Description**: Enormous sentient tree corrupted by a parasitic entity
- **Phases**:
  1. Dormant: Attacks with roots while stationary
  2. Awakened: Uproots and pursues players
  3. Purification/Corruption: Players choose to purify or destroy, affecting rewards
- **Special Mechanics**: Destructible limbs change attack patterns
- **Unique Rewards**: Heartwood Staff (purification) or Blighted Thorns (corruption)

### Campaign Bosses

#### General Kargath
- **Role**: Scourge military leader opposed to peace
- **Encounter**: Multi-stage battle throughout a Scourge fortress
- **Special Mechanics**: Commands troops during the fight, battlefield changes
- **Significance**: Represents the militaristic faction of the Scourge
- **Unique Rewards**: Kargath's Battle Plans (unlock special missions)

#### High Inquisitor Velastra
- **Role**: Sentinel zealot hunting "heretics" who cooperate with the Scourge
- **Encounter**: Chase sequence followed by multi-phase magical battle
- **Special Mechanics**: Creates illusions, manipulates light to blind players
- **Significance**: Represents extremism within the Sentinel faction
- **Unique Rewards**: Purified Crystal (powerful crafting material)

### Final Boss

#### The Worldender
- **Description**: Ancient entity that exists between realities
- **Arena**: Fragmented reality with platforms floating in the void
- **Phases**:
  1. The Harbinger: Uses avatars to attack while building power
  2. Reality Breaker: Warps the environment and physics
  3. True Form: Massive cosmic entity with devastating attacks
  4. Sealing: Cooperative phase requiring specific actions from all heroes
- **Special Mechanics**: 
  - Reality shifts that change game rules temporarily
  - Requires using the Fragments of Creation at specific moments
  - Different for each hero, showcasing their unique abilities
- **Unique Rewards**: 
  - Epilogue content
  - Ability to reset talents
  - Access to special post-game content
