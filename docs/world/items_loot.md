# Items & Loot

## Equipment System

### Weapons

#### Weapon Types
- **Swords**: Balanced weapons with moderate damage and speed
  - One-handed swords can be paired with off-hand items
  - Two-handed swords deal more damage but reduce mobility
  - Special variants include runic blades and elemental swords
  
- **Axes**: High damage weapons with cleaving attacks
  - One-handed axes focus on critical hits
  - Two-handed axes have area damage potential
  - Special variants include berserker axes and executioner blades
  
- **Maces & Hammers**: Armor-piercing weapons with stun potential
  - One-handed maces are fast but lower damage
  - Two-handed hammers can break enemy defenses
  - Special variants include holy maces and thunder hammers
  
- **Daggers**: Fast weapons that enable critical strike builds
  - Can be dual-wielded for rapid attacks
  - Emphasize backstab and poison mechanics
  - Special variants include ritual daggers and phase blades
  
- **Staves**: Magical weapons that enhance spellcasting
  - Provide significant bonuses to magical abilities
  - Can channel elemental energies for basic attacks
  - Special variants include ancient staves and living wood staves
  
- **Bows & Crossbows**: Ranged weapons with different firing mechanics
  - Bows emphasize rate of fire and mobility
  - Crossbows focus on power and penetration
  - Special variants include compound bows and repeating crossbows

#### Weapon Properties
- **Base Damage**: The fundamental damage value
- **Attack Speed**: How quickly attacks can be performed
- **Critical Chance**: Probability of landing a critical hit
- **Critical Damage**: Multiplier applied on critical hits
- **Range**: Effective distance for attacks
- **Special Effects**: Unique properties like elemental damage, lifesteal, etc.
- **Scaling**: How the weapon improves with hero attributes

#### Weapon Customization
- **Enchantment Slots**: Sockets for magical enhancements
- **Upgrade Paths**: Specialization options when improving weapons
- **Appearance Variations**: Visual customization options
- **Legendary Transformations**: Special quests to unlock weapon potential

### Armor

#### Armor Slots
- **Head**: Helmets, circlets, masks
- **Chest**: Breastplates, robes, tunics
- **Hands**: Gauntlets, gloves, bracers
- **Legs**: Greaves, pants, skirts
- **Feet**: Boots, sandals, sabatons
- **Shoulders**: Pauldrons, spaulders, mantles
- **Back**: Cloaks, capes, backpacks

#### Armor Types
- **Light Armor**: Emphasizes mobility and evasion
  - Cloth: Highest magical bonuses, lowest physical protection
  - Leather: Balanced protection with stealth benefits
  - Scale: Moderate protection with minimal movement penalties
  
- **Medium Armor**: Balanced protection and mobility
  - Chain: Flexible protection against slashing attacks
  - Lamellar: Balanced protection with moderate weight
  - Banded: Stronger protection with some magical properties
  
- **Heavy Armor**: Maximum protection at the cost of mobility
  - Plate: Highest physical protection, significant weight
  - Stone: Unique properties like status immunity
  - Crystal: Magical protection with special properties

#### Armor Properties
- **Physical Defense**: Reduction of incoming physical damage
- **Magical Defense**: Reduction of incoming magical damage
- **Weight**: Affects movement speed and stamina consumption
- **Durability**: How quickly the armor degrades with use
- **Special Resistances**: Protection against specific damage types
- **Set Bonuses**: Additional effects when wearing multiple pieces
- **Enchantment Capacity**: Ability to hold magical enhancements

### Accessories

#### Accessory Types
- **Rings**: Small but powerful items with focused effects
  - Limit of two equipped simultaneously
  - Effects range from attribute bonuses to special abilities
  - Some legendary rings have unique activation effects
  
- **Amulets**: Worn around the neck with broad protective effects
  - Only one can be equipped at a time
  - Often provide resistances and defensive bonuses
  - Ancient amulets may contain trapped spirits or powers
  
- **Trinkets**: Miscellaneous items with unique properties
  - Carried in special inventory slots
  - Often have active abilities with cooldowns
  - Examples include pocket watches, figurines, and charms
  
- **Relics**: Powerful ancient items with significant effects
  - Only one can be active at a time
  - Provide unique abilities not available elsewhere
  - Often tied to specific factions or historical events

#### Accessory Properties
- **Attribute Bonuses**: Direct increases to hero attributes
- **Proc Effects**: Chance-based triggers on certain actions
- **Active Abilities**: Special powers that can be activated
- **Passive Auras**: Constant effects that benefit the hero
- **Conditional Bonuses**: Effects that activate under specific circumstances
- **Synergy Effects**: Bonuses when used with certain other items

## Consumables

### Potions

#### Health & Resource Potions
- **Minor Healing Potion**: Restores 25% health instantly
- **Major Healing Potion**: Restores 50% health instantly
- **Superior Healing Potion**: Restores 75% health instantly
- **Minor Mana Potion**: Restores 25% mana instantly
- **Major Mana Potion**: Restores 50% mana instantly
- **Superior Mana Potion**: Restores 75% mana instantly
- **Rejuvenation Potion**: Restores both health and mana over time

#### Buff Potions
- **Potion of Strength**: Temporarily increases Strength attribute
- **Potion of Agility**: Temporarily increases Agility attribute
- **Potion of Intelligence**: Temporarily increases Intelligence attribute
- **Potion of Vitality**: Temporarily increases Vitality attribute
- **Potion of Spirit**: Temporarily increases Spirit attribute
- **Elixir of the Warrior**: Increases physical damage and defense
- **Elixir of the Mage**: Increases magical damage and mana regeneration

#### Utility Potions
- **Potion of Invisibility**: Grants temporary invisibility
- **Potion of Speed**: Increases movement speed for a short duration
- **Antidote**: Cures poison effects
- **Elixir of Clarity**: Removes confusion and mind control effects
- **Frost Resistance Potion**: Provides temporary immunity to cold
- **Fire Resistance Potion**: Provides temporary immunity to fire
- **Potion of Water Breathing**: Allows breathing underwater

### Scrolls

#### Combat Scrolls
- **Scroll of Fireball**: Unleashes a powerful fireball at target location
- **Scroll of Chain Lightning**: Strikes multiple enemies with lightning
- **Scroll of Frost Nova**: Freezes all nearby enemies
- **Scroll of Earthquake**: Damages and knocks down enemies in an area
- **Scroll of Binding**: Immobilizes a target temporarily
- **Scroll of Summoning**: Calls a temporary ally to fight alongside the hero
- **Scroll of Banishment**: Removes summoned creatures from battle

#### Utility Scrolls
- **Scroll of Identification**: Reveals the properties of an unknown item
- **Scroll of Town Portal**: Creates a portal to the nearest settlement
- **Scroll of Repair**: Restores durability to damaged equipment
- **Scroll of Enchantment**: Temporarily enhances a weapon or armor piece
- **Scroll of Mapping**: Reveals a portion of the current area map
- **Scroll of Treasure Finding**: Highlights nearby treasure for a duration
- **Scroll of Recall**: Teleports the hero to a previously marked location

## Unique Artifacts

### Legendary Weapons

#### Sentinel Artifacts
- **Radiant Edge**: Sword that channels pure light energy
  - Passive: Attacks pierce magical defenses
  - Active: Creates a blade of pure light that extends range
  - History: Forged by the first Sentinel champion to fight the darkness
  
- **Nature's Wrath**: Bow that grows its own arrows
  - Passive: Arrows cause entangling vines on hit
  - Active: Summons a volley of seeking thorns
  - History: Grown from the World Tree's first branch

#### Scourge Artifacts
- **Soulreaver**: Axe that harvests spiritual energy
  - Passive: Absorbs a portion of defeated enemies' essence
  - Active: Releases stored energy in a devastating attack
  - History: Created by the first Scourge overlord from a fallen god's bone
  
- **Void Caller**: Staff that manipulates dimensional energy
  - Passive: Spells have a chance to create temporary rifts
  - Active: Opens a portal to summon shadowy allies
  - History: Discovered in the ruins of an ancient civilization that vanished mysteriously

#### Neutral Artifacts
- **Peacemaker**: Hammer that grows stronger when used for defense
  - Passive: Blocking attacks charges the weapon
  - Active: Releases stored energy as a shockwave
  - History: Forged by artisans from both factions during a brief alliance
  
- **Equilibrium**: Paired daggers representing balance
  - Passive: Alternating attacks between daggers increases damage
  - Active: Split the user's shadow into a temporary fighting clone
  - History: Created by a hero who walked between both factions

## Loot System

### Drop Mechanics

#### Enemy Drops
- Common enemies have a standard loot table with basic items
- Elite enemies have guaranteed special drops
- Bosses have specific loot tables with unique items
- Higher difficulty enemies have better quality drops
- Some enemies drop faction-specific items

#### Environmental Loot
- Treasure chests with randomized contents
- Resource nodes for gathering crafting materials
- Hidden caches requiring exploration or puzzle-solving
- Destructible objects that may contain items
- Ancient technology that can be salvaged

#### Quest Rewards
- Guaranteed specific items for quest completion
- Choice between multiple rewards for some quests
- Unique items only available through specific quests
- Reputation-gated rewards from faction quests
- Scaling rewards based on player level

### Rarity System

#### Rarity Tiers
- **Common (White)**: Basic items with no special properties
- **Uncommon (Green)**: Slightly enhanced items with minor bonuses
- **Rare (Blue)**: Quality items with one special property
- **Epic (Purple)**: Powerful items with multiple special properties
- **Legendary (Orange)**: Unique items with significant effects and lore
- **Artifact (Red)**: One-of-a-kind items with game-changing properties

#### Rarity Effects
- Higher rarity items have better base stats
- More special properties appear at higher rarities
- Higher rarity items have more customization options
- Visual effects become more elaborate with higher rarity
- Legendary and Artifact items have unique models and effects

### Special Item Types

#### Set Items
- Pieces of equipment that provide bonuses when worn together
- Each faction has multiple sets with different focuses
- Set bonuses activate at 3 and 5 pieces equipped
- Some sets are split between different heroes
- Completing sets often requires specific challenges

#### Cursed Items
- Powerful items with significant drawbacks
- Can be purified through special quests or rituals
- Some curses can be beneficial in certain situations
- Certain heroes may be immune to specific curses
- Some cursed items transform when certain conditions are met

#### Living Equipment
- Items that grow stronger as they are used
- Develop new properties based on how they are used
- Can form bonds with specific heroes
- May have personalities that affect gameplay
- Some can transform permanently after significant events
