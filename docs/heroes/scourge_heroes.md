# Scourge Heroes

## Axe (Mogul Khan)

### Character Overview
- **Full Name**: Mogul Khan
- **Title**: The Axe
- **Faction**: Scourge
- **Hero Type**: Warrior/Tank
- **Difficulty**: ★★☆☆☆ (Beginner-Friendly)
- **Primary Attribute**: Strength

### Background
Born in the harsh wastelands of the Red Mist Horde, Mogul Khan rose through the ranks of his warlike people through sheer brutality and combat prowess. After slaying his own commanding officer for showing weakness, Khan declared himself the new general—or "Axe"—of the horde. His uncompromising leadership and battlefield ferocity earned him legendary status among his people.

When his tribe was conquered by the Scourge, Khan initially resisted but came to respect their strength and philosophy of power through any means. Now he serves as one of the Scourge's most formidable warriors, reveling in battle and seeking worthy opponents to test his might against. He cares little for politics or subtlety, preferring the direct approach of cleaving his enemies in two.

### Personality
- **Traits**: Aggressive, straightforward, battle-hungry, prideful
- **Motivations**: Proving his strength, finding worthy challenges, earning glory
- **Fears**: Weakness, dishonor, dying an unremarkable death
- **Relationships**: Respects strength in others, speaks of himself in the third person

### Visual Design
- **Appearance**: Massive, red-skinned warrior with imposing physique
- **Attire**: Minimal armor with tribal markings, skull trophies, and war paint
- **Weapon**: Massive battle axe that glows with a subtle red energy
- **Distinguishing Features**: Red skin, mohawk, battle scars across body
- **Animation Style**: Powerful, weighty movements with aggressive posturing

### Abilities

#### Passive: Counter Helix
- **Effect**: When attacked, Axe has a chance to perform a spinning counter-attack that damages all nearby enemies
- **Mechanics**: Proc chance increases with Strength
- **Visual**: Rapid spin with axe extended, creating a red energy circle
- **Strategic Use**: Positioning to maximize number of enemies hit when it triggers

#### Q: Berserker's Call
- **Type**: Area of Effect Taunt
- **Effect**: Forces nearby enemies to attack Axe while granting him bonus armor
- **Cooldown**: 16 seconds
- **Resource Cost**: 80 rage
- **Scaling**: Duration and armor bonus increase with ability points
- **Visual**: Axe roars with a visible shockwave, enemies glow red when taunted
- **Strategic Use**: Initiating fights, protecting allies, setting up Counter Helix

#### W: Battle Hunger
- **Type**: Single Target Damage Over Time/Buff
- **Effect**: Places a curse on an enemy that deals damage over time and slows them until they kill a unit; grants Axe movement speed
- **Cooldown**: 20 seconds
- **Resource Cost**: 60 rage
- **Scaling**: Damage, slow, and speed boost increase with ability points
- **Visual**: Target is surrounded by swirling red energy
- **Strategic Use**: Harassing enemies, chasing fleeing targets, maintaining rage in combat

#### E: Culling Blade
- **Type**: Single Target Execution
- **Effect**: Attempts to instantly kill a low-health enemy; if successful, grants Axe and nearby allies a speed boost
- **Cooldown**: 75/65/55 seconds (reduces with tier)
- **Resource Cost**: 120 rage
- **Scaling**: Health threshold increases with ability points
- **Visual**: Overhead axe slam with blood splash effect on successful execution
- **Strategic Use**: Finishing weakened enemies, securing kills, momentum in team fights

#### R: Bloodthirst (Ultimate)
- **Type**: Self Buff/Transformation
- **Effect**: Axe enters a berserker rage, gaining attack speed, movement speed, and causing his attacks to cleave multiple enemies
- **Cooldown**: 90 seconds
- **Resource Cost**: 150 rage
- **Scaling**: Duration and bonus effects increase with ability points
- **Visual**: Axe grows slightly larger, glows red, and leaves blood trails when moving
- **Strategic Use**: Maximizing damage output in team fights, rapid clearing of enemy groups

### Playstyle

#### Combat Role
- **Primary Function**: Tank/Initiator who thrives in the middle of combat
- **Positioning**: Front line, surrounded by as many enemies as possible
- **Strengths**: Durability, crowd control, threat generation
- **Weaknesses**: Limited range, vulnerable to kiting, rage management

#### Ability Combos
- **Initiation Combo**: Berserker's Call → Counter Helix procs → Culling Blade to finish
- **Chase Combo**: Battle Hunger to slow → Pursuit → Culling Blade execution
- **Ultimate Engagement**: Berserker's Call → Bloodthirst → Cleaving attacks on grouped enemies

#### Resource Management
- **Rage Generation**: Builds when dealing or taking damage
- **Rage Conservation**: Maintaining enough for Berserker's Call in case of surprise encounters
- **Item Synergy**: Equipment that increases rage generation or reduces ability costs

### Progression Path

#### Ability Advancement Priority
1. Berserker's Call (primary engagement tool)
2. Counter Helix (passive damage)
3. Battle Hunger (utility and chase)
4. Culling Blade (situational execution)

#### Recommended Talent Paths
- **Tank Focus**: Enhances durability and Counter Helix reliability
- **Damage Focus**: Improves Culling Blade and Bloodthirst effectiveness
- **Mobility Focus**: Emphasizes Battle Hunger and movement capabilities

#### Key Equipment
- **Early Game**: Durability items, rage generation
- **Mid Game**: Initiation tools, armor enhancement
- **Late Game**: Area damage items, execution power enhancement

### Lore Connections

#### Key Relationships
- **Disruptor**: Former tribal rival, now uneasy ally in the Scourge
- **Centaur Warrunner**: Respected opponent from past battles
- **Crystal Maiden**: Views her as weak but is surprised by her resilience

#### Story Involvement
- Central to storylines involving Scourge military campaigns
- Key character in quests about tribal politics and warfare
- Represents the more straightforward, combat-focused aspect of the Scourge

#### Personal Quest Line
"The Red Mist Rising" - A five-part quest series exploring Axe's past as a tribal leader and his rise to power, culminating in a confrontation with a former rival that tests his loyalty to the Scourge.

## Lich (Kel'Thuzad)

### Character Overview
- **Full Name**: Kel'Thuzad
- **Title**: The Lich
- **Faction**: Scourge
- **Hero Type**: Mage/Crowd Control
- **Difficulty**: ★★★☆☆ (Intermediate)
- **Primary Attribute**: Intelligence

### Background
Once a respected mage and scholar, Kel'Thuzad's insatiable thirst for forbidden knowledge led him down a dark path. His studies of death magic and necromancy made him an outcast among his peers, but the Scourge recognized his potential. Through a ritual that cost him his humanity, Kel'Thuzad transformed himself into a lich—an undead being of immense magical power.

Now existing between life and death, Kel'Thuzad serves the Scourge with his mastery of frost and necromantic magic. He views his undead state not as a curse but as transcendence beyond mortal limitations. Cold and calculating, he approaches problems with the patience of one who no longer fears the passage of time.

### Personality
- **Traits**: Calculating, patient, arrogant, darkly humorous
- **Motivations**: Acquiring knowledge, perfecting his magic, achieving greater power
- **Fears**: Destruction of his phylactery (the vessel containing his soul)
- **Relationships**: Mentors promising Scourge mages, disdainful of the living

### Visual Design
- **Appearance**: Skeletal undead figure with glowing blue eyes
- **Attire**: Ornate robes with frost patterns, chains with magical symbols
- **Weapon**: Elaborate staff topped with a skull surrounded by floating ice shards
- **Distinguishing Features**: Constant frost aura, hovering slightly above ground
- **Animation Style**: Unnatural movements with floating limbs, spectral effects

### Abilities

#### Passive: Frost Armor
- **Effect**: Automatically encases Lich in protective ice that slows attackers
- **Mechanics**: Activates when attacked, scales with Intelligence
- **Visual**: Ice crystals forming around body, flash of cold when struck
- **Strategic Use**: Trading attacks advantageously, deterring melee attackers

#### Q: Frost Nova
- **Type**: Area of Effect Damage/Slow
- **Effect**: Releases a burst of cold that damages and slows enemies in a target area
- **Cooldown**: 8 seconds
- **Resource Cost**: 125 mana
- **Scaling**: Damage and slow intensity increase with ability points
- **Visual**: Explosion of ice crystals from the ground
- **Strategic Use**: Area control, slowing enemy advances, setting up other abilities

#### W: Dark Ritual
- **Type**: Resource Management/Sacrifice
- **Effect**: Sacrifices a portion of Lich's health to restore a larger amount of mana
- **Cooldown**: 45 seconds
- **Resource Cost**: 20% current health
- **Scaling**: Mana restored increases with ability points
- **Visual**: Dark energy transferring from body to staff
- **Strategic Use**: Maintaining mana in extended fights, emergency resource generation

#### E: Ice Chains
- **Type**: Single Target Control/Damage
- **Effect**: Binds an enemy in chains of ice, rooting them and dealing damage over time
- **Cooldown**: 12 seconds
- **Resource Cost**: 100 mana
- **Scaling**: Duration and damage increase with ability points
- **Visual**: Spectral chains of ice emerging from the ground to bind target
- **Strategic Use**: Locking down priority targets, interrupting channeled abilities

#### R: Chain Frost (Ultimate)
- **Type**: Bouncing Area Damage
- **Effect**: Launches a ball of frost that bounces between enemies, dealing damage and slowing with each hit
- **Cooldown**: 120 seconds
- **Resource Cost**: 200 mana
- **Scaling**: Damage, number of bounces, and slow effect increase with ability points
- **Visual**: Massive ice orb that leaves frost trails as it bounces
- **Strategic Use**: Maximum damage against grouped enemies, area control in team fights

### Playstyle

#### Combat Role
- **Primary Function**: Control mage with area damage and crowd control
- **Positioning**: Mid-range, close enough to control the battlefield but away from direct danger
- **Strengths**: Powerful crowd control, area damage, mana sustainability
- **Weaknesses**: Limited mobility, health management with Dark Ritual, vulnerable to burst damage

#### Ability Combos
- **Control Combo**: Ice Chains to root → Frost Nova for additional slow and damage
- **Ultimate Setup**: Frost Nova to slow enemies → Chain Frost for maximum bounces
- **Survival Sequence**: Frost Armor activation → Dark Ritual for mana → Frost Nova to create distance

#### Resource Management
- **Mana Sustainability**: Using Dark Ritual strategically to maintain spell casting
- **Health Management**: Balancing health sacrifices with defensive positioning
- **Item Synergy**: Items that provide health regeneration or maximum health to offset Dark Ritual costs

### Progression Path

#### Ability Advancement Priority
1. Frost Nova (primary damage and control)
2. Ice Chains (single target control)
3. Dark Ritual (resource management)
4. Chain Frost (situational ultimate)

#### Recommended Talent Paths
- **Control Focus**: Enhances crowd control duration and effectiveness
- **Damage Focus**: Improves frost damage and Chain Frost bounces
- **Sustainability Focus**: Reduces Dark Ritual health cost and improves Frost Armor

#### Key Equipment
- **Early Game**: Mana regeneration items, health sustainability
- **Mid Game**: Crowd control enhancement, defensive items
- **Late Game**: Area damage amplification, ultimate enhancement items

### Lore Connections

#### Key Relationships
- **Crystal Maiden**: Respects her mastery of ice but sees her as naive
- **Necrophos**: Fellow practitioner of death magic with different approaches
- **Invoker**: Ancient rivalry from when both were living mages

#### Story Involvement
- Central to storylines involving necromancy and the undead
- Key character in quests about forbidden knowledge
- Represents the more intellectual and magical aspect of the Scourge

#### Personal Quest Line
"Secrets of Undeath" - A five-part quest series exploring Lich's transformation and his search for ancient magical artifacts, culminating in a ritual that can either strengthen his undead form or begin a path to redemption.

## Storm Spirit (Raijin Thunderkeg)

### Character Overview
- **Full Name**: Raijin Thunderkeg
- **Title**: The Storm Spirit
- **Faction**: Scourge (Reluctant/Independent)
- **Hero Type**: Mobile Mage/Assassin
- **Difficulty**: ★★★★☆ (Advanced)
- **Primary Attribute**: Intelligence

### Background
Raijin was once a jovial brewmaster whose curiosity led him to capture what he thought was ordinary lightning in a special keg. The lightning was actually a primordial storm spirit, and in the resulting explosion, Raijin's body and the spirit merged into a single being. Now part mortal and part elemental, Raijin retains his cheerful personality while wielding the awesome power of the storm.

Though aligned with the Scourge due to their acceptance of his chaotic nature, Storm Spirit maintains a level of independence unusual for the faction. He serves more as an ally of convenience than a true believer in the Scourge philosophy, using their resources while pursuing his own agenda of exploration and experiencing the thrill of the storm.

### Personality
- **Traits**: Exuberant, thrill-seeking, unpredictable, witty
- **Motivations**: Experiencing new sensations, testing the limits of his powers
- **Fears**: Being contained or restricted, losing his elemental half
- **Relationships**: Friendly with most, but forms few deep connections due to his restless nature

### Visual Design
- **Appearance**: Rotund blue-skinned humanoid crackling with electricity
- **Attire**: Eastern-inspired clothing with storm motifs, ceremonial brewmaster's belt
- **Weapon**: Channels lightning directly through his hands
- **Distinguishing Features**: Electrical aura, hair that stands on end, eyes that glow during abilities
- **Animation Style**: Quick, bouncy movements with occasional electrical discharges

### Abilities

#### Passive: Overload
- **Effect**: After using an ability, Storm Spirit's next attack deals bonus damage and slows the target
- **Mechanics**: Buff appears after any ability use, consumed on next attack
- **Visual**: Storm Spirit glows with intense blue electricity
- **Strategic Use**: Weaving attacks between abilities for maximum damage

#### Q: Static Remnant
- **Type**: Area Trap/Damage
- **Effect**: Creates an electrical duplicate that explodes when enemies come near
- **Cooldown**: 3.5 seconds
- **Resource Cost**: 70 mana
- **Scaling**: Damage and detection radius increase with ability points
- **Visual**: Crackling blue copy of Storm Spirit
- **Strategic Use**: Area control, checking bushes, setting traps

#### W: Electric Vortex
- **Type**: Single Target Control
- **Effect**: Creates a vortex that pulls an enemy toward Storm Spirit
- **Cooldown**: 18 seconds
- **Resource Cost**: 85 mana
- **Scaling**: Pull duration increases with ability points
- **Visual**: Swirling electrical currents that drag the target
- **Strategic Use**: Isolating targets, interrupting channeled abilities, positioning enemies

#### E: Lightning Dash
- **Type**: Mobility
- **Effect**: Storm Spirit dashes forward in a surge of lightning, damaging enemies he passes through
- **Cooldown**: 6 seconds
- **Resource Cost**: 60 mana
- **Scaling**: Distance, damage, and cooldown reduction increase with ability points
- **Visual**: Blue lightning streak with Storm Spirit reforming at the end point
- **Strategic Use**: Repositioning in combat, escaping danger, chasing enemies

#### R: Ball Lightning (Ultimate)
- **Type**: Global Mobility/Damage
- **Effect**: Storm Spirit transforms into a ball of lightning that travels to a target location, damaging enemies along the path
- **Cooldown**: 12 seconds
- **Resource Cost**: Initial mana cost plus cost per distance traveled
- **Scaling**: Damage increases and mana cost decreases with ability points
- **Visual**: Massive lightning ball with Storm Spirit visible inside
- **Strategic Use**: Long-distance travel, dramatic repositioning in fights, escape mechanism

### Playstyle

#### Combat Role
- **Primary Function**: Mobile assassin who darts in and out of combat
- **Positioning**: Constantly changing, using mobility to find advantageous angles
- **Strengths**: Unparalleled mobility, good single-target damage, elusive
- **Weaknesses**: Mana dependency, vulnerable when abilities are on cooldown, fragile

#### Ability Combos
- **Initiation Combo**: Ball Lightning in → Electric Vortex to pull → Static Remnant → Overload-empowered attack
- **Escape Sequence**: Static Remnant to deter pursuit → Lightning Dash or Ball Lightning to safety
- **Chase Combo**: Lightning Dash to close gap → Electric Vortex to prevent escape → Overload attacks

#### Resource Management
- **Mana Conservation**: Careful use of Ball Lightning due to distance-based cost
- **Regeneration Focus**: Prioritizing mana regeneration in equipment choices
- **Item Synergy**: Items that provide mana pool expansion and regeneration

### Progression Path

#### Ability Advancement Priority
1. Lightning Dash (mobility and safety)
2. Static Remnant (farming and damage)
3. Electric Vortex (control)
4. Ball Lightning (situational ultimate)

#### Recommended Talent Paths
- **Mobility Focus**: Enhances Lightning Dash and Ball Lightning efficiency
- **Damage Focus**: Improves Overload and Static Remnant damage
- **Control Focus**: Strengthens Electric Vortex and adds control effects to other abilities

#### Key Equipment
- **Early Game**: Mana regeneration, basic damage items
- **Mid Game**: Mobility enhancement, mana pool expansion
- **Late Game**: Burst damage items, cooldown reduction

### Lore Connections

#### Key Relationships
- **Ember Spirit and Earth Spirit**: Fellow elemental entities with different origins
- **Disruptor**: Rival storm manipulator with more controlled approach
- **Leshrac**: Philosophical discussions about chaos and nature

#### Story Involvement
- Central to storylines involving elemental forces
- Key character in quests about balance and chaos
- Represents the unpredictable and free-spirited aspect of the Scourge

#### Personal Quest Line
"Eye of the Storm" - A five-part quest series exploring Storm Spirit's dual nature and his search for other elemental spirits, culminating in a choice between embracing his human side or fully becoming an elemental entity.
