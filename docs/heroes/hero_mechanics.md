# Hero Mechanics

## Hero Structure

### Base Attributes

#### Primary Attributes
- **Strength**: Determines physical power and health
  - Each point increases physical damage by 1
  - Each point increases maximum health by 10
  - Each point increases physical defense by 0.5
  - Primary attribute for Warriors and Tanks

- **Agility**: Determines speed and precision
  - Each point increases attack speed by 1%
  - Each point increases dodge chance by 0.2%
  - Each point increases movement speed by 0.5%
  - Primary attribute for Assassins and Rangers

- **Intelligence**: Determines magical power and resource pool
  - Each point increases magical damage by 1.5
  - Each point increases maximum mana by 8
  - Each point increases magical defense by 0.5
  - Primary attribute for Mages and Supports

- **Vitality**: Determines health regeneration and resistance
  - Each point increases health regeneration by 0.2 per second
  - Each point increases status effect resistance by 0.5%
  - Each point increases maximum health by 15
  - Secondary attribute for all heroes

- **Spirit**: Determines resource regeneration and critical strikes
  - Each point increases resource regeneration by 0.1 per second
  - Each point increases critical strike chance by 0.3%
  - Each point increases cooldown reduction by 0.5%
  - Secondary attribute for all heroes

#### Attribute Distribution
- Each hero has a unique starting attribute distribution
- Heroes gain attributes automatically when leveling up
- Attribute growth rates vary by hero type
- Equipment provides additional attribute bonuses
- Certain abilities and talents can permanently increase attributes

### Hero Types

#### Warrior
- **Focus**: Front-line combat and physical damage
- **Resource**: Rage (builds during combat, decays when out of combat)
- **Strengths**: High health, strong melee damage, crowd control
- **Weaknesses**: Limited range, vulnerable to kiting, resource management
- **Playstyle**: Aggressive engagement, managing rage generation and spending
- **Example**: Axe (Mogul Khan)

#### Assassin
- **Focus**: Burst damage and mobility
- **Resource**: Energy (regenerates quickly, limited maximum)
- **Strengths**: High single-target damage, mobility, stealth
- **Weaknesses**: Fragile, vulnerable when abilities are on cooldown
- **Playstyle**: Strategic engagement, target selection, timing attacks
- **Example**: Future hero - Phantom Assassin (Mortred)

#### Mage
- **Focus**: Magical damage and area control
- **Resource**: Mana (large pool, slow regeneration)
- **Strengths**: Powerful spells, area damage, utility
- **Weaknesses**: Channeling requirements, mana management
- **Playstyle**: Positioning, spell combinations, resource management
- **Example**: Storm Spirit (Raijin Thunderkeg)

#### Support
- **Focus**: Healing, buffs, and crowd control
- **Resource**: Mana (balanced pool and regeneration)
- **Strengths**: Team enhancement, healing, defensive abilities
- **Weaknesses**: Lower personal damage, reliance on allies
- **Playstyle**: Ally protection, timing crucial interventions
- **Example**: Crystal Maiden (Rylai)

#### Specialist
- **Focus**: Unique mechanics and situational power
- **Resource**: Varies (often unique to the hero)
- **Strengths**: Specialized abilities, unique gameplay mechanics
- **Weaknesses**: Situational effectiveness, complexity
- **Playstyle**: Mastery of unique mechanics, adaptation to circumstances
- **Example**: Lich (Kel'Thuzad)

### Faction Alignment

#### Sentinel (Order, Protection, Light)
- **Philosophy**: Preservation of order and protection of the natural world
- **Aesthetic**: Bright colors, organic shapes, nature motifs
- **Strengths**: Healing, defensive abilities, light magic
- **Weaknesses**: Less raw power, more reliance on cooperation
- **Heroes**: Crystal Maiden, future heroes like Omniknight, Enchantress

#### Scourge (Chaos, Power, Darkness)
- **Philosophy**: Embracing change and pursuing power through any means
- **Aesthetic**: Dark colors, sharp angles, intimidating designs
- **Strengths**: Raw damage, corruption effects, dark magic
- **Weaknesses**: Self-damage mechanics, higher risk abilities
- **Heroes**: Axe, Lich, future heroes like Shadow Fiend, Dazzle

#### Faction Interactions
- Heroes from opposing factions have unique dialogue when in the same party
- Certain locations react differently to heroes based on faction
- Some quests have different approaches based on faction alignment
- Certain equipment has enhanced effects for specific factions
- Some abilities have synergies or conflicts based on faction

## Ability System

### Ability Structure

#### Active Abilities
- Each hero has 4 active abilities (Q, W, E, R keys)
- Ultimate ability (R) is more powerful with longer cooldown
- Active abilities have cooldowns, resource costs, and targeting types
- Abilities can be enhanced through leveling and talent choices
- Some abilities have charge systems or toggle effects

#### Passive Abilities
- Each hero has 1 innate passive ability
- Passives provide constant effects that define the hero's playstyle
- Some passives have activation conditions or stacking effects
- Passives can interact with active abilities for enhanced effects
- Certain items and talents can modify passive abilities

#### Ability Components
- **Cooldown**: Time before ability can be used again
- **Resource Cost**: Mana, energy, rage, or other resource required
- **Targeting Type**: Self, target, direction, area, or global
- **Effect**: Damage, healing, crowd control, or utility
- **Scaling**: How the ability improves with attributes or level

### Ability Types

#### Damage Abilities
- **Direct Damage**: Immediate damage to target(s)
- **Damage Over Time**: Continuing damage over a duration
- **Area Damage**: Affects multiple targets in an area
- **Conditional Damage**: Enhanced damage under specific conditions
- **Scaling Damage**: Increases based on missing health, distance, etc.

#### Crowd Control Abilities
- **Stun**: Prevents all actions for duration
- **Root**: Prevents movement but allows other actions
- **Slow**: Reduces movement speed
- **Silence**: Prevents ability usage
- **Disarm**: Prevents basic attacks
- **Fear**: Forces target to run away
- **Taunt**: Forces target to attack the caster

#### Utility Abilities
- **Movement**: Dashes, teleports, or speed boosts
- **Shielding**: Absorbs incoming damage
- **Stealth**: Concealment from enemies
- **Transformation**: Changes hero form or abilities
- **Summoning**: Creates allied units
- **Terrain Manipulation**: Creates or modifies terrain
- **Status Cleansing**: Removes debuffs or crowd control

#### Buff/Debuff Abilities
- **Attribute Enhancement**: Increases attributes temporarily
- **Damage Amplification**: Increases damage dealt or taken
- **Regeneration**: Increases health or resource recovery
- **Vulnerability**: Creates weaknesses to specific damage types
- **Auras**: Affects all allies or enemies in an area
- **Stacking Effects**: Builds up power with repeated application

### Ability Advancement

#### Ability Points
- Earned primarily through level-up (1 per level)
- Additional points from special quests and rare items
- Maximum of 40 ability points available per hero
- Points can be redistributed at special locations for a cost

#### Ability Tiers
Each ability has three tiers of advancement:

- **Tier 1 (1 Point)**
  - Base functionality of the ability
  - Moderate damage/effect
  - Standard cooldown and resource cost

- **Tier 2 (2 Additional Points)**
  - Enhanced damage/effect (approximately +50%)
  - Reduced cooldown (approximately -20%)
  - Additional minor effect (e.g., slow, brief stun)
  - Improved area of effect or range

- **Tier 3 (3 Additional Points)**
  - Significantly enhanced damage/effect (approximately +100% from base)
  - Further reduced cooldown (approximately -40% from base)
  - Additional major effect (e.g., piercing damage, longer stun)
  - Fundamental improvement to ability functionality

#### Ability Modifications
- Special items can permanently modify abilities
- Certain talents can alter how abilities function
- Environmental factors can temporarily enhance abilities
- Hero-specific quests can unlock hidden potential in abilities

## Talent System

### Talent Structure

#### Talent Paths
Each hero has three distinct talent paths representing different playstyles:

- **Path 1: Offensive Focus**
  - Emphasizes damage output and aggressive playstyle
  - Examples: increased critical damage, penetrating attacks, reduced offensive cooldowns

- **Path 2: Defensive Focus**
  - Emphasizes survivability and control
  - Examples: damage reduction, crowd control duration, shield generation

- **Path 3: Utility Focus**
  - Emphasizes versatility and resource management
  - Examples: cooldown reduction, resource regeneration, movement abilities

#### Talent Tiers
- Five talent tiers unlocked at levels 5, 10, 15, 20, and 25
- One point to spend at each tier
- Points can be redistributed at special locations for a cost
- Each tier offers a choice between the three paths
- Higher tiers provide more impactful talents

### Talent Types

#### Passive Enhancements
- Permanent stat increases
- New passive effects
- Modifications to existing passive abilities
- Conditional bonuses based on combat situations
- Threshold effects that activate at certain health/resource levels

#### Ability Modifications
- Cooldown reduction for specific abilities
- Resource cost reduction
- Additional effects on existing abilities
- Altered targeting or area of effect
- Fundamental changes to how abilities function

#### New Abilities
- Additional active abilities beyond the base four
- Situational abilities with specific use cases
- Transformation abilities that change the hero temporarily
- Toggle abilities that switch between different effects
- Reactive abilities that trigger automatically under conditions

#### Playstyle Modifiers
- Changes to basic attack behavior
- Alterations to resource generation or consumption
- Movement or control modifications
- Interaction changes with environment or objects
- Special effects when interacting with specific enemy types

### Talent Examples

#### Axe (Mogul Khan) Talents
- **Offensive Path**
  - Level 5: "Battle Hunger" - Gain rage when taking damage
  - Level 10: "Cleaving Strikes" - Basic attacks hit in a small arc
  - Level 15: "Executioner" - Culling Blade resets all cooldowns on kill
  - Level 20: "Berserker's Rage" - Gain attack speed when below 50% health
  - Level 25: "Unstoppable Force" - Immune to movement impairments while Berserker's Call is active

- **Defensive Path**
  - Level 5: "Hardened Skin" - Increase physical defense
  - Level 10: "Counter Force" - Counter Helix has a chance to stun
  - Level 15: "Battle Trance" - Gain health regeneration while in combat
  - Level 20: "Intimidating Presence" - Enemies deal less damage while near Axe
  - Level 25: "Last Stand" - Automatically activate Berserker's Call when health falls below 20%

- **Utility Path**
  - Level 5: "Battlefield Momentum" - Gain movement speed after killing an enemy
  - Level 10: "War Cry" - New ability that buffs nearby allies
  - Level 15: "Tactical Advance" - Gain a short dash ability
  - Level 20: "Battle Rhythm" - Basic attacks reduce ability cooldowns
  - Level 25: "Warlord's Command" - Berserker's Call affects a larger area and reduces enemy damage output

#### Crystal Maiden (Rylai) Talents
- **Offensive Path**
  - Level 5: "Frost Bite" - Crystal Nova slows attack speed
  - Level 10: "Ice Shards" - Basic attacks have a chance to freeze
  - Level 15: "Arctic Blast" - Freezing Field deals increasing damage over time
  - Level 20: "Shatter" - Frozen enemies take increased damage
  - Level 25: "Winter's Wrath" - Freezing Field creates ice shards that deal additional damage

- **Defensive Path**
  - Level 5: "Ice Armor" - Gain damage reduction
  - Level 10: "Frost Barrier" - Create ice walls that block enemies
  - Level 15: "Frozen Heart" - Reduce nearby enemy movement speed
  - Level 20: "Glacial Embrace" - Frost Bite can be cast on allies to protect them
  - Level 25: "Absolute Zero" - Freezing Field grants damage immunity

- **Utility Path**
  - Level 5: "Crystalline Clarity" - Increase mana regeneration aura
  - Level 10: "Flash Freeze" - Reduce all ability cooldowns
  - Level 15: "Ice Path" - Create a path of ice that increases movement speed
  - Level 20: "Winter's Blessing" - New ability that enhances nearby allies' abilities
  - Level 25: "Arcane Mastery" - Spells have a chance to cost no mana

## Hero Selection & Switching

### Initial Selection

#### Starting Hero Choice
- Players choose one of four initial heroes at the beginning of the game
- Each hero represents a different playstyle and faction
- Initial tutorial is tailored to the chosen hero's abilities
- Starting equipment and abilities are hero-specific
- Initial quests introduce the hero's backstory and motivations

#### Hero Introduction
- Cinematic introduction to the chosen hero
- Tutorial section teaching basic abilities
- Introduction to the hero's faction and allies
- Initial quest chain establishing the hero's place in the world
- Gradual unlocking of abilities during the introduction

### Hero Switching

#### Unlock Mechanism
- Hero switching becomes available after completing the introduction
- Initial switch occurs at a story moment when heroes first meet
- Additional heroes are unlocked through main story progression
- Some heroes require special quests to become playable
- Heroes from opposing factions become available later in the story

#### Switch Locations
- Sentinel and Scourge Sanctuaries in major settlements
- Campfires in the wilderness (limited selection)
- Special mirrors found throughout the world
- Hero monuments in significant locations
- Personal quarters in faction headquarters

#### Switching Mechanics
- Heroes retain their individual levels and equipment
- Abilities and talents are preserved when switching
- Current quest progress is maintained across heroes
- Some areas or quests are hero-specific or faction-restricted
- Certain combinations of heroes unlock special dialogue or interactions

### Hero Relationships

#### Dialogue System
- Heroes have unique conversations with NPCs
- Different dialogue options based on hero personality
- Special interactions between heroes from the same faction
- Tense or hostile exchanges between opposing faction heroes
- Relationship development through shared experiences

#### Relationship Development
- Heroes develop relationships based on quest choices
- Friendship, rivalry, or romance can develop between certain heroes
- Relationship status affects combat bonuses when heroes fight together
- Special quests become available based on relationship status
- Some story decisions require balancing relationships between multiple heroes

#### Hero-Specific Content
- Each hero has unique side quests
- Personal storylines that develop character arcs
- Hero-specific reactions to major story events
- Unique interactions with certain NPCs or locations
- Special abilities or items only available to specific heroes
