# Hero Skill Specialization

## Overview

Each hero in Legends of the Ancient Realms has been designed with a unique set of skills that define their playstyle and combat capabilities. This document outlines the specialized abilities for each hero and how they contribute to the hero's overall theme and role.

## Axe (Mogul Khan)

Axe is a berserker tank who thrives in the midst of battle, drawing enemies to attack him while punishing them for doing so. His abilities focus on taunting enemies, counterattacking, and executing low-health foes.

### Unique Abilities

1. **Berserker's Call** (Key 1)
   - Taunts nearby enemies and increases armor while entering a battle rage
   - Grants temporary attack speed boost
   - Creates a visual rage aura around Axe

2. **Battle Hunger** (Key 2)
   - Enrages an enemy with hunger for battle, causing damage over time
   - Reduces the target's attack damage
   - Grants Axe movement speed while the debuff is active on an enemy

3. **Counter Helix** (Key 3)
   - Passive ability that triggers a counterattack when Axe is hit
   - Each successful counter grants a stack of Berserker's Fury, increasing attack damage
   - Stacks up to 5 times, making Axe more dangerous the longer he fights

4. **Culling Blade** (Key 4)
   - Instantly executes low-health enemies
   - On successful execution, grants attack and movement speed to Axe and nearby allies
   - Cooldown resets on successful execution, allowing for potential multi-kills

5. **War Cry** (Key 5)
   - Increases armor and movement speed
   - Reduces incoming damage
   - Intimidates nearby enemies, reducing their attack damage

6. **Berserker's Rage** (Key 6)
   - Toggle ability that sacrifices health for increased damage and attack speed
   - Automatically deactivates when health gets too low
   - Visual rage aura indicates the active state

### Playstyle

Axe excels at initiating fights and drawing enemy attention. His abilities allow him to:
- Tank damage for the team
- Control multiple enemies with taunts
- Execute weakened foes to create momentum in team fights
- Become more powerful the longer he stays in combat

## Crystal Maiden (Rylai)

Crystal Maiden is a frost mage who specializes in area control and support. Her abilities focus on freezing enemies, providing utility to allies, and unleashing devastating ice storms.

### Unique Abilities

1. **Crystal Nova** (Key 1)
   - Damages and slows enemies in an area
   - Creates a lingering frost field that continues to damage enemies
   - Perfect for zoning and area control

2. **Frostbite** (Key 2)
   - Encases an enemy in ice, preventing movement and attacks
   - Applies "Brittle" debuff, increasing magical damage taken
   - Excellent for setting up combos with other magical damage dealers

3. **Arcane Brilliance** (Key 3)
   - Passive aura that provides mana regeneration to all allies
   - Reduces cooldowns of nearby allies' abilities
   - Grants Crystal Maiden spell amplification, enhancing her magical damage

4. **Freezing Field** (Key 4)
   - Summons a devastating blizzard that damages enemies
   - Damage and slow effects intensify over time
   - Random chance to freeze enemies caught in the storm
   - Grants Crystal Maiden armor while channeling

5. **Glacial Barrier** (Key 5)
   - Creates a protective ice barrier that increases armor
   - Reflects frost damage to attackers
   - Releases a burst of cold when the barrier breaks, slowing nearby enemies

6. **Frost Nova** (Key 6)
   - Releases a wave of frost in all directions
   - Creates a ring of ice shards that block enemy movement
   - Excellent for escape or creating space in team fights

### Playstyle

Crystal Maiden excels at supporting allies and controlling areas with frost magic. Her abilities allow her to:
- Provide mana and cooldown reduction to the team
- Control enemy movement with freezing effects
- Deal significant area damage over time
- Create defensive barriers for herself and allies

## Lich (Kel'Thuzad)

Lich is an undead sorcerer who combines frost magic with necromantic powers. His abilities focus on draining life, applying powerful debuffs, and unleashing devastating chain frost attacks.

### Unique Abilities

1. **Frost Nova** (Key 1)
   - Damages enemies with necromantic frost
   - Drains life force, healing Lich for a portion of the damage dealt
   - Combines offensive power with self-sustain

2. **Ice Carapace** (Key 2)
   - Encases the target in necromantic ice, increasing armor and spell resistance
   - Curses attackers, slowing their attack and movement speed
   - More defensive than Crystal Maiden's similar ability

3. **Soul Sacrifice** (Key 3)
   - Sacrifices a friendly unit to restore mana and gain spell amplification
   - Can be cast on enemy corpses to restore health
   - Embodies the necromantic theme of sacrificing life for power

4. **Chain Frost** (Key 4)
   - Unleashes a powerful orb that bounces between enemies
   - Damage increases with each bounce
   - Applies stacking slow effect
   - Chance to freeze targets, adding unpredictability to the ability

5. **Necrotic Frost** (Key 5)
   - Creates an explosion of necrotic energy
   - Applies "Necrotic Wound" debuff, reducing healing received
   - Excellent counter against healing-focused enemies

6. **Frozen Tomb** (Key 6)
   - Creates a protective barrier that absorbs damage
   - Spawns frost spirits when destroyed that seek out and damage enemies
   - Combines defense with delayed offensive capability

### Playstyle

Lich excels at sustained damage and debuffing enemies. His abilities allow him to:
- Drain life from enemies to sustain himself
- Apply powerful debuffs that weaken enemies
- Control areas with frost effects
- Sacrifice units for personal power
- Counter healing strategies

## Storm Spirit (Raijin Thunderkeg)

Storm Spirit is a mobile caster who harnesses the power of lightning. His abilities focus on mobility, area damage, and empowering his attacks with electrical energy.

### Unique Abilities

1. **Static Remnant** (Key 1)
   - Creates an electrical duplicate that damages nearby enemies
   - Reveals affected enemies' locations
   - Grants movement speed when triggered
   - Excellent for scouting and area control

2. **Electric Vortex** (Key 2)
   - Pulls an enemy toward Storm Spirit
   - Stuns the target upon arrival
   - Slows nearby enemies
   - Perfect for isolating targets

3. **Overload** (Key 3)
   - Passive ability that empowers attacks after using abilities
   - Deals bonus damage in an area around the target
   - Each consecutive Overload attack increases damage
   - Grants attack speed with each Overload attack

4. **Ball Lightning** (Key 4)
   - Transforms into lightning to travel quickly
   - Grants invulnerability during travel
   - Each enemy hit reduces the mana cost
   - Grants movement speed after use
   - Signature mobility skill

5. **Lightning Rush** (Key 5)
   - Charges forward, damaging enemies in a line
   - Gains attack speed for each enemy hit
   - Cooldown reduction when hitting multiple enemies
   - Combines mobility with offensive capability

6. **Electric Field** (Key 6)
   - Creates an energized field that damages enemies
   - Increases attack and movement speed of allies
   - Storm Spirit gains double benefits while inside
   - Excellent for team fights and area control

### Playstyle

Storm Spirit excels at mobility and burst damage. His abilities allow him to:
- Quickly reposition in fights
- Deal area damage to multiple enemies
- Empower attacks after ability use
- Support allies with speed buffs
- Control areas with electrical fields

## Skill Differentiation

Each hero's abilities have been carefully designed to support their unique playstyle while maintaining balance:

- **Axe** focuses on physical damage, taunting, and becoming stronger in prolonged fights
- **Crystal Maiden** specializes in frost-based crowd control and team support
- **Lich** combines frost with necromancy for debuffs and life drain
- **Storm Spirit** emphasizes mobility and lightning-based area damage

This differentiation ensures that each hero feels unique to play and contributes differently to team compositions.
